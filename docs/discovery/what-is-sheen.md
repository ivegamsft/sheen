# What is sheen?

sheen is the design governance finish coat for basecoat. It packages the
design-layer assets that teams reach for most often: skills, agents, tokens,
instructions, and themed outputs.

## What it does

- gives consumers a controlled sync model through `.sheen.yml`
- keeps design language, token usage, and workflow guidance consistent on the
  design/UI files where each instruction is actionable
- helps designers, engineers, and platform teams find the right asset fast

## What it is not

- not a replacement for the normative spec
- not a random grab bag of prompts
- not a one-size-fits-all config; adoption should scale with need
