# Discovery and research contract

Read/apply this contract before using the [brief](../templates/discovery-brief.md)
or [register](../templates/evidence-findings.md). They are blank starter assets,
not evidence or instructions to conduct a study. Store completed artifacts only
at the requested downstream destination; agree the recipient and sharing scope.

## Plan first

Capture client/product context, decision to inform, success/acceptance criteria,
scope and exclusions, existing decisions and constraints, audience, recruitment
and accessibility needs, authorized methods, roles, schedule and outputs.
Separate stakeholder assertions from user observations. Write neutral interview
questions about actual context, tasks, barriers and workarounds, not desired
answers. Include a usability protocol when requested and authorized: task,
starting condition, success criterion, observation method and stop condition.
Preserve research planning, persona synthesis, usability testing and governance
review as available capabilities within the requested and authorized scope.
Do not expand a discovery brief into new research, persona synthesis, usability
testing or governance review that was not requested or authorized. Mark excluded
activities not applicable with a scope reason; gaps become proposed follow-ups,
not permission to conduct a study.

Assign stable `A-`, `U-`, `E-`, `F-` and `D-` IDs to assumptions, unknowns,
evidence, findings and decisions. An assumption is an unverified working belief;
an unknown is a gap/question. Both need impact, validation method/question,
owner and checkpoint. Explicitly say "none identified" only after review.
With no authorized evidence, output a plan and hypotheses, not a completed study.

## Permission and privacy gate

Before collection/use, record purpose, voluntary informed consent or applicable
documented authorization, participant withdrawal process, and separately granted
recording permission. Record who may access/share which material, approved
storage, retention period, deletion owner and limitations. Unknown permission
blocks the affected activity. A supplied source or client approval alone does
not prove participant consent or permission for another purpose.

Do not contact participants, record, scrape, publish or transfer data without
explicit authorization. Minimize/redact shared deliverables; use pseudonymous
IDs and approved locators, not names, contact details, credentials, recordings
or identifying quotes. Keep raw evidence in its approved store. Do not assert
anonymity or legal compliance just because identifiers were removed. Escalate
unclear policy, sensitive populations/data and conflicting permissions to the
responsible owner; never invent consent or approve an undocumented exception.
On withdrawal, remove evidence from active synthesis and reassess dependent
findings; retain only policy-permitted non-sensitive change history.

## Traceability and synthesis

Each evidence record includes source ID, authorized reference with exact
locator (section, timestamp or excerpt location), date/version, method/context,
availability and authorization/restrictions. Cite approved minimized summaries
where raw material cannot be shared. No claim of source access if it was not
actually available. Missing, inaccessible, stale, withdrawn or unauthorized
support makes a claim unresolved or a hypothesis, never evidence-backed.

For each finding, distinguish observation, interpretation and recommendation.
Link `E-` IDs and locators; record confidence with rationale (no invented
statistical precision), limitations, contradictory evidence and unresolved
conflicts. Link assumptions/unknowns; report severity/impact against explicit
criteria, remediation owner, closure criteria and escalation threshold.
Do not silently drop inconvenient evidence or infer prevalence/representativeness
from qualitative anecdotes. Quotes must be exact, authorized and safely shared;
otherwise paraphrase with attribution and label as a summary, not a quote.
Never fabricate interviews, participants, quotes, metrics, consent or validation.
Personas cite supporting findings and limits. Hypothetical personas stay labeled
hypotheses and cannot stand in for observed participants.

## Coherent handoff

Include brief revision/scope, `E-`/`F-`/`A-`/`U-`/`D-` IDs, authorized locators,
restrictions, accepted decisions, conflicts, receiving owner, next validation
and acceptance checkpoint. Keep raw participant data out by default.

- `design-bootstrap`: greenfield token/theme, component and IA foundations.
- `design-exploration`: divergent concepts grounded in findings, not sign-off.
- `information-architecture`: content grouping/structure.
- `web-usability-review`: heuristic-only evaluation, not participant evidence.
- `experience-blueprint` / `wireframing`: journey/index and screen/flow artifacts.
- `ux-designer`: coordinates research findings into design decisions.

Findings do not authorize implementation, override accepted decisions or grant
final design approval. Publish the governance report and decision log including
policy exceptions, owners, closure criteria and follow-up checkpoints.
