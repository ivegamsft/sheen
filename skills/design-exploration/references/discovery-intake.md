# Discovery intake for divergent concepts

Apply this intake before [creative calibration](creative-calibration.md).
Read the supplied brief revision/scope, audience/context, accepted decisions,
evidence and finding IDs/authorized locators, assumptions, unknowns, conflicts
and sharing restrictions. Route interview/discovery brief preparation, research
planning, persona synthesis and usability protocols to `user-research`.

Separate observed evidence, stakeholder assertions and hypotheses. No evidence
packet means provisional concept exploration with explicit gaps, not a completed
study. Record assumption/unknown IDs, impact, validation owner and checkpoint.
Missing, stale, withdrawn, inaccessible or unauthorized support cannot justify
an evidence-backed claim; reassess affected concept rationale.
Never invent interviews, quotes, metrics, consent, source access or validation.
Do not contact, record, publish or transfer participant data without explicit
authorization. Keep raw participant data in its approved store; share only
minimized authorized locators/summaries. Missing permission blocks evidence use,
not hypothesis-labeled exploration.

Map each direction to supporting finding IDs or labeled hypotheses. Preserve
creative calibration: subject/audience/primary-job/context grounding, memorable
focus with restraint, anti-template comparison and rejected-alternatives log.
Accepted decisions remain authoritative; concepts are not final sign-off.
Pass brief revision, evidence/finding and assumption/unknown IDs, limitations,
restrictions, conflicts, risks/dependencies, receiving owner and next validation/
acceptance checkpoint to `design-debate` or the downstream design owner.
