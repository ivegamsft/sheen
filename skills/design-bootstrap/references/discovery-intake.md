# Discovery intake for greenfield foundations

Before seeding foundations, inspect the supplied brief revision/scope, accepted
decisions, evidence/finding IDs and authorized locators, assumptions, unknowns,
conflicts and sharing restrictions. No discovery packet is required to begin
a provisional bootstrap: record missing inputs as unknowns, assign validation
owners/checkpoints and label affected token/theme/IA choices provisional.

`user-research` owns client interview/discovery briefs, research planning,
interview/persona synthesis, usability protocols and evidence registers. Request
that handoff when evidence is needed; do not duplicate interviewing or invent
user validation. Client assertions are stakeholder inputs, not observed research.
Do not contact, record, publish or transfer participant data without explicit
authorization. Accept only minimized authorized evidence; raw participant data
stays in its approved store. Missing permission blocks use of that evidence.

Carry finding and assumption/unknown IDs into the foundation rationale and
staged rollout: recipient, restrictions, next validation and acceptance checkpoint.
Preserve accepted brand/platform decisions. Retain core/semantic tokens,
light/dark/high-contrast themes, component/spec conventions, IA/navigation,
accessibility/usability/content guardrails and rollout ownership.
Research findings are not implementation authorization or final design sign-off.
